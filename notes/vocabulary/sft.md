# SFT

SFT is usually the first step of [post-training](post-training.md). You collect prompts with high-quality responses, written by people or generated by other models and then filtered, and continue training a [base model](base-model.md) to produce those responses. The loss is the same next-token loss as in [pretraining](pretraining.md), usually counted only on the response, not on the prompt.

It's called *supervised* because every example comes with the answer to imitate, unlike [RL](rl.md), where the model only gets a score. Datasets range from about a thousand carefully chosen examples to millions.

SFT is an efficient way to teach format, tone and instruction following. Because the model imitates its examples, though, it's limited by their quality, which is one reason SFT is usually followed by [RLHF](rlhf.md), [DPO](dpo.md) or [RLVR](rlvr.md).

**Example:** one training example.

```text
Prompt:   Explain what a GPU is in one sentence.
Response: A GPU is a processor with thousands of small cores that run many calculations in parallel, which makes it well suited to graphics and to training neural networks.
```
